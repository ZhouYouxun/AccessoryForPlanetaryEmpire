This mod will add some accessories to the game that can be obtained in the early stages (and at various times)
but will increase the player's value in percentage, so they can be used until graduation.
Currently, this mod will only focus on accessories.
If you have any comments, please add me as a steam friend for feedback.
I sincerely welcome anyone to give me any feedback or come up with some ideas to make my mod more interesting!

This mod mainly refers to the battleship defense skills of some bullet-hell shooting games, and designs many equipments. 
They can often increase the player's movement speed, or defense, or eliminate the enemy's bullets, or give the player a certain invincibility time
But these will have a certain price. 
(Many of the current equipment) The inspiration mainly comes from Phoenix2 (a game)

Important: Due to my bad English, all the English translations in the game are machine translated. I am sorry if this has caused you any trouble.

As of version 6.1, there are:
21 general offensive accessories
8 professional skill offensive accessories (active or passive skills)
16 professional basic offensive accessories (only numerical improvement)
5 sprint accessories
26 defensive accessories
3 mobility accessories
3 logistics accessories
1 living accessories
6 weapons

QOL content:
14 tools
100+ original item modification recipes
